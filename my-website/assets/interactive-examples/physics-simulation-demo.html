How Physics Simulation Works:

  • Gravity: Pulls objects downward at a constant rate
  • Collision Detection: Prevents objects from passing through each other
  • Friction: Slows objects as they move across surfaces
  • Inertia: Objects resist changes in motion
  • Realistic Motion: Objects accelerate and decelerate naturally
