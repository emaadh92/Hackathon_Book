Key Aspects of Human-Robot Interaction:

  • Safety First: Robots maintain safe distances from humans
  • Natural Behavior: Robots follow social conventions
  • Clear Communication: Robot intentions are visible to humans
  • Adaptive Response: Robots adjust behavior based on human actions
  • Trust Building: Predictable behavior increases human confidence
