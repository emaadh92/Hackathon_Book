## Short Answer Questions

11. Explain the importance of visual realism in digital worlds for both robot perception and human comfort.

12. Describe three ways that virtual humans can be represented in digital world simulations.

## Essay Question

16. Discuss the balance between computational efficiency and fidelity in digital world design. What trade-offs must be considered?

## Answers

7. b) False

9. b) False

11. Visual realism is important for robot perception because robots rely on visual sensors that respond to realistic textures, colors, and shapes. For human comfort, realistic appearance helps humans form appropriate expectations and interact more intuitively with robots.

12. (1) Avatar-based: 3D models representing human appearance and movement, (2) Behavioral: Models simulating human decision-making and actions, (3) Social: Models capturing social norms and interaction patterns.

13. Key components include: daily activities (routine behaviors), emergency responses (how humans react to unusual situations), social interactions (conversations, cooperation), and adaptive behavior (changing responses based on robot actions).

14. Visual feedback helps by showing attention heatmaps, decision trees, prediction displays, and uncertainty indicators. This makes robot thought processes visible to humans, enhancing transparency and trust.

15. (1) Collaborative tasks: Humans and robots working together, (2) Service interactions: Robots providing assistance or services, (3) Educational settings: Robots as teachers or learning companions, (4) Care situations: Robots assisting elderly or disabled individuals.

16. The balance involves trade-offs between visual and physical accuracy versus computational resources. High fidelity requires more processing power, memory, and time. Designers must prioritize which elements are most important for the intended use case, selectively applying detail where it matters most for interaction quality while maintaining reasonable performance. Efficient algorithms and adaptive rendering help optimize this balance.
